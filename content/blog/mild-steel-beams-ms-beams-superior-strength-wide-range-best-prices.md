## Understanding Mild Steel Beams

Mild Steel Beams are fundamental structural components in the construction industry, renowned for their strength, versatility, and cost-effectiveness.

### Characteristics of Mild Steel Beams

-   **Material:** Made from mild steel, known for its excellent **ductility, weldability, and strength**.
-   **Weight:** Varies significantly based on dimensions and type, typically ranging from **13 kg/m to over 54 kg/m** for I-Beams and can be even heavier for H-Beams.
-   **Dimensions:** Available in various lengths (commonly between **3 to 12 meters**) and thicknesses, providing flexibility in construction designs.

### Types of Mild Steel Beams

We offer the following main types of MS Beams:

1.  **I-Beams:**
    -   Characterized by their I-shaped cross-section.
    -   Commonly used in structural applications.
    -   Available in various sizes, such as 127 x 76 mm, 305 x 127 mm, with weights ranging from 13 kg/m to over 54 kg/m .
    -   Extremely versatile and used in a wide array of building and construction projects.
2.  **H-Beams:**
    -   Distinguished by their H-shaped cross-section.
    -   Suitable for heavy loads and often used in the construction of large buildings, bridges, and other infrastructure projects.
    -   Provide enhanced load-bearing capacity compared to I-Beams.
3.  **Universal Beams (UB):**
    -   Standardized sizes and often used interchangeably with I-Beams.
    -   Offer high strength-to-weight ratios, making them ideal for structural support.
    -   Provide excellent structural integrity and are widely used in various construction applications.

## Mild Steel Beams: Common Grades

We supply MS Beams in various grades, including:

-   **E250 BR:** The most widely used grade, offering a good balance of strength, ductility, and weldability.
-   **E350 BR:** Provides higher strength compared to E250 BR, suitable for applications demanding greater load-bearing capacity.
-   **IS 2062:** The prominent Indian Standard for structural steel, ensuring the beams meet specific quality and performance criteria.
